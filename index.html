How to Use AssessmentHub

  1. Enter your assessment details in the form.
  2. Click Add Assessment.
  3. Use the dashboard to view your workload.
  4. Check the colour labels to see if a task is overdue, due soon, upcoming or complete.
  5. Use filters to view tasks by subject or status.
